Welcome to your business world

CommerceVerse turns Class 11 Commerce into a place you can walk around in. Visit a city of real businesses, test your accounting, move a market with a slider, read about Diwali across India, and then try to escape the quiz room.

This project follows the Class 11 GSEB Commerce course, in English medium. Pick any module in any order. There is nothing to sign up for.

  • Organisation of CommerceExplore public, private and global enterprises in Business City.
  • AccountancyPractise debit and credit rules in the Accounts Lab.
  • EconomicsChange demand and supply in the Market Simulator.
  • EnglishRead and learn figures of speech in the English Studio.

How are enterprises classified here?

Ownership tells you who owns and controls the enterprise. In the public sector, the Central or a State Government owns and controls it. In the private sector, individuals or shareholders own it.

Reach tells you where it does business. A global enterprise (multinational) operates in more than one country. Reach is not a type of ownership: every global enterprise in this city is also owned by someone, and its profile says who.

A company whose shares can be bought on a stock exchange is called a listed company. Being listed does not make a company public sector. What matters is who holds the controlling ownership.
